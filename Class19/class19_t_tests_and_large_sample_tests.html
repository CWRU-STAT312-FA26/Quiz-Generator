t-Tests and Large-Sample Mean Tests

Distinguish exact t inference from the specified large-sample normal approximation.

Normal-population t inference is exact at any sample size. The t examples here use small samples. A sample size greater than 30 alone does not establish the accuracy of a normal approximation.

The statistic is the observed mean minus the null mean, divided by SE. For a two-sided test use the absolute statistic and both tails. Reject when p≤αp\le\alpha. A failure to reject does not prove the null.

Critical-value inputs use left cumulative area: alpha for a left-tailed test, 1 - alpha for a right-tailed test, and 1 - alpha/2 for the positive two-sided critical magnitude. A two-sided test matches a 100(1−α)%100(1-\alpha)\% interval based on the same distribution.
