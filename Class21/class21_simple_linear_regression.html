Study guide for fitting a line

The classical model uses a linear conditional mean and independent, mean-zero errors with constant variance. Normal errors give the exact small-sample t reference for inference.

Fit the line with β̂1=Sxy/Sxx,β̂0=y‾−β̂1x‾\hat\beta_1=S_{xy}/S_{xx},\quad \hat\beta_0=\bar y-\hat\beta_1\bar x. Fitted values and residuals are ŷi=β̂0+β̂1xi,ei=yi−ŷi\hat y_i=\hat\beta_0+\hat\beta_1x_i,\quad e_i=y_i-\hat y_i. Estimate error variance with MSE=SSE/(n−2)MSE=SSE/(n-2).
