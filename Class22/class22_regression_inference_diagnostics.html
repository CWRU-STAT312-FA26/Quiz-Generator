Study guide for slope inference and diagnostics

For the classical slope test and interval, estimate the error variance with MSE=SSE/(n−2)MSE=SSE/(n-2) and the slope's standard error with SÊ(β̂1)=MSE/Sxx\widehat{SE}(\hat\beta_1)=\sqrt{MSE/S_{xx}}. Under the model, use T=(β̂1−β1,0)/SÊ(β̂1)T=(\hat\beta_1-\beta_{1,0})/\widehat{SE}(\hat\beta_1) with n−2n-2 degrees of freedom. The matching two-sided interval is β̂1±t*SÊ(β̂1)\hat\beta_1\pm t^*\widehat{SE}(\hat\beta_1).

In summary(lm(...)), use the predictor row for its coefficient, standard error, t statistic, and two-sided p-value. confint() reports the limits for the specified coefficient and confidence level.

Read a residual plot for changes in spread or curvature. Use a normal Q–Q plot to assess normality and collection order or study design to assess independence.
